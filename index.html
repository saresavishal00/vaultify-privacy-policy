Vaultify Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Vaultify is a private vault behind a working calculator. It keeps your photos, videos, notes and downloads encrypted on your own phone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

1. What stays on your phone

  • Your photos, videos, notes and downloads are encrypted (AES-256) and stored only on your phone. The encryption key is kept in Android's secure Keystore.
  • Your PINs and recovery code are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, never as the numbers themselves.
  • Vaultify has no accounts and no servers. The developer never receives your files, notes, PINs or what you browse.
  • Android's cloud backup is turned off for Vaultify. Backups you make yourself are encrypted with a password you choose and saved only where you choose, for example your own Google Drive.
  • Deleted items stay in the app's recycle bin for 7 days (30 days with Premium), then they're removed for good.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

2. Information the developer collects

None. Vaultify has no analytics or crash reporting of its own, and nothing you store in it is sent to the developer.

3. Google services

Vaultify uses these Google services, which handle information under Google's own policies:

  • Google AdMob (free version only). Ads are shown inside the vault, never on the calculator. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with the ads. Ads never see what's in your vault. With Premium, the ads SDK isn't started at all. How Google uses this information.
  • Google User Messaging Platform. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you're asked for consent before any ads are requested. Your choice is kept on your phone, and you can change it any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings.
  • Google Play Billing. Google Play processes Premium payments. Vaultify never sees your payment details; it only asks Google Play which plan your Google account has, and remembers the answer on your phone.
  • Google Play In-App Review. Vaultify may show Google Play's own card for rating the app.

4. The built-in browser

Websites you open receive your IP address and whatever you send them, under their own privacy policies. Their cookies and site data stay inside Vaultify on your phone until you clear them in the browser; history and cache are cleared when the vault closes. Files you download are encrypted into your vault.

5. Permissions

  • Internet and network state, for the browser, ads and Google Play.
  • Photos and files are chosen with Android's own picker, so Vaultify never gets access to your whole gallery. An original is only deleted after you confirm it in Android's own dialog.
  • Fingerprint and face unlock use Android's own prompt. Vaultify never receives your biometric data.

6. Security

Besides encryption, Vaultify blocks screenshots and screen recording of the app, and locks the vault as soon as you leave it. No way of storing data is perfectly secure, so keep your phone's own screen lock on too.

7. Your PIN and recovery code

If you forget your PIN, you can set a new one with the 12-digit recovery code shown when you set up the app. The developer can't recover your PIN, recovery code or backup password for you.

8. Children

Vaultify isn't directed at children under 13, and the developer doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may change as Vaultify does. The effective date at the top shows the latest version, and the app shows the same policy in Settings → Privacy policy.
