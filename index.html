Vaultify Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026

Thank you for using Vaultify. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Vaultify collects, how it is used, and how your data is protected.

1. Information We Collect

Vaultify is designed with privacy as a core principle.

  • Your photos, documents, notes, downloads and other files remain stored locally on your device.
  • Vaultify does not require you to create an account.
  • Vaultify does not upload your private vault contents to our servers.
  • Your encrypted vault data remains on your own device.

2. Device Permissions

Vaultify requests only the permissions necessary for its features to function. These permissions may include:

  • Storage access to import and export files.
  • Media access for selecting photos or other supported content.
  • Internet access for the built-in secure browser and application updates.

Permissions are used solely to provide the requested functionality.

3. Data Storage

Vaultify stores encrypted user data locally on your device. Encryption and decryption occur directly on your device whenever possible.

Vaultify does not maintain cloud copies of your private vault files.

4. Security

Vaultify uses encryption and secure local storage techniques designed to help protect your private content. However, no software or storage method can guarantee complete security. Users are responsible for protecting their device, screen lock, and Vault PIN.

5. PIN Responsibility

Vaultify currently does not provide PIN recovery. If you forget your PIN, access to encrypted data may not be recoverable. Users are responsible for remembering their PIN before storing important information.

6. Downloaded Content

Vaultify includes a secure browser that allows downloading supported content for personal use.

Users are responsible for ensuring they have permission from the original copyright owner before redistributing, publishing, or re-uploading downloaded content. Vaultify does not grant ownership or redistribution rights for downloaded material.

7. Third-Party Services

Vaultify may use trusted third-party services for features such as:

  • Google Play Billing
  • Google AdMob (Free version only)
  • Android System Components

These services operate under their own privacy policies.

8. Children's Privacy

Vaultify is not specifically intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Vaultify evolves. Changes become effective immediately after this page is updated. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.
